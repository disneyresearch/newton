Size `SolverKamino` contact buffers with one per-world rule for PADMM and DVI, and for both internal collision detection and `CollisionPipeline` contacts: each world gets the sum of its shape-pair contact limits, capped at the larger of 1000 and the `CollisionPipeline` capacity estimate for that world. `config.collision_detector.max_contacts_per_world` and `max_contacts` now also apply when `use_collision_detector` is disabled; integral floats such as `1e5` are converted to integers, and fractional values raise a `TypeError`. Dense PADMM scenes can receive less capacity than before, and DVI and `CollisionPipeline` contacts no longer give every world the same capacity; set `config.collision_detector.max_contacts_per_world` to choose a fixed per-world capacity.
