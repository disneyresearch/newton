Treat an existing `Model.rigid_contact_max`, including an explicit `0`, as an upper bound for `SolverKamino` contact capacity. Kamino scales its per-world capacities down to fit, warning only if a world with colliding shape pairs is left without capacity, and sets `rigid_contact_max` to its own total only when it is `None`, once construction succeeds. Construct the solver before the `CollisionPipeline`, or raise `rigid_contact_max`, to keep Kamino's full capacity.
