Reserve 255 contacts, the most that Newton's contact reduction keeps for one shape pair, for each `SolverKamino` shape pair that involves a mesh or heightfield, instead of 32 or 64, within the per-world `CollisionPipeline` estimate. Convex-mesh pairs keep their previous limits. A world with several mesh or heightfield pairs therefore usually receives that estimate, at least 1000 contacts, and dense dynamics allocate memory that grows with the square of the per-world capacity; set `config.collision_detector.max_contacts_per_world` or use `sparse_dynamics=True` to bound it.
